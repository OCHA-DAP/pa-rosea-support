Kenya Humanitarian Fund flood triggers: when they would have been reached

The Kenya Humanitarian Fund's RA2 allocation (September 2026) sets aside USD 4 million for anticipatory action ahead of El Niño floods, with partner triggers on KMSA rainfall forecasts. This page shows every time since 1998 that observed rainfall reached each threshold, and whether a flood was recorded in that county at the time.

IMERG rainfall 1998-2026-09-28 | EM-DAT floods 1998-2024 | return periods over 28 years | built 2026-09-30
  • 150 mm in 7 days was reached 7 times in Mandera, Wajir and Marsabit. 6 were during a flood EM-DAT had already recorded in that county; 0 came before one.
  • 70 mm in 7 days over the Ewaso Ng'iro area was reached in 23 of 28 years. 32 of its 46 activations had no recorded flood in Isiolo or Samburu.
  • 40 mm in a day over the upper Tana was reached in 19 of 28 years and caught 12 of 24 recorded floods downstream.
  • At least one trigger as written would have been reached in 24 of 28 years.

Not covered

  • Forecast skill. Observed rainfall stands in for the KMSA forecast; a forecast of the same total would be issued up to 7 days earlier.
  • The gauge triggers: Garissa Bridge 5.1 m (Kenya Red Cross Society), Flood Alert at Garissa 3.0-3.5 m (Dadaab partners), and the Danish Refugee Council 24-hour trigger for Darika, which gives no rainfall amount.
  • How the thresholds apply over space. The document does not say. This page uses the county average; read at the wettest point in the county, 150 mm is reached in 25 or more of 28 years.
Method and data
  • Rainfall: NASA IMERG Late Run v7, daily, 0.1 degree, 1998-01-01 to 2026-09-28. County boundaries: Kenya COD-AB.
  • Activation: the first day the running total reaches the threshold; the next 30 days count as the same activation. 1-in-3 and 1-in-5 thresholds are set on the annual maximum of the same indicator.
  • Floods: EM-DAT events whose location names the trigger's counties, 1998 to 2024. "Before": an event starts within 30 days after the activation. "During": an event was already under way. EM-DAT dates are often season-long and one event can list many counties.
  • Return periods: Weibull, (n + 1) / years reached, over 28 full years (1998-2025).
  • County averages match the team database table public.imerg (correlation 0.9989, median difference 0.03 mm). Mandera 2000 is 149.6 mm here and 150.7 mm in the database.
