Kenya flood triggers: how often the rainfall thresholds are reached in October to December

Different data source: results may varyThe triggers are written on forecasts from KMSA (Kenya Meteorological Service Authority). This page uses NASA's IMERG satellite rainfall estimates instead. Totals from the two sources differ, so the dates and counts here may not match what KMSA data would give.
Two ways to read a rainfall threshold
County average: rainfall averaged over the whole county or area. The Welthungerhilfe trigger says "average"; the others do not say.
Single cell: at least one satellite grid cell, about 11 km across, reaches the threshold somewhere in the county or area.

At a glance

October to December seasons, 1998 to 2025.

  • Mandera, Wajir and Marsabit (150 mm in 7 days): Reached in at least one of the three counties in 3 of 28 seasons on the county average, and 22 of 28 at a single cell.
  • Isiolo (70 mm in 7 days): Reached in 11 of 28 seasons on the county average, and 26 of 28 at a single cell. At 100 mm: 6 and 22.
  • Samburu (70 mm in 7 days): Reached in 10 of 28 seasons on the county average, and 26 of 28 at a single cell. At 100 mm: 6 and 22.
  • Garissa (40 mm in a day, rainfall part): Reached in 11 of 28 seasons on the upper Tana average, and 28 of 28 at a single cell.
  • All triggers together, as written: At least one was reached in 15 of 28 seasons on the average, and 28 of 28 at a single cell. This uses the Welthungerhilfe trigger as written, averaged over six counties.
  • Other counties: In the eight ASAL counties named in the allocation, 150 mm in 7 days was reached in 0 to 3 of 28 seasons on the county average, and 12 to 24 at a single cell.

What this page does not cover

  • Whether KMSA forecasts would have predicted these totals. This page uses observed rainfall.
  • The river-level triggers: Garissa Bridge above 5.1 m (Kenya Red Cross Society), Flood Alert levels at Garissa, Hola and Garsen (Dadaab partners), and the Danish Refugee Council trigger for Darika, which has no rainfall amount.
  • Which reading KMSA forecasts or the partners use. Both are shown because the document only specifies it for one trigger.
Method and data
  • Season: only rainfall totals whose last day falls between 1 October and 31 December count, and only EM-DAT floods that started in those months. The 28 seasons are 1998-2025; the October to December 2026 season is not yet in the record.
  • Rainfall: NASA IMERG Late Run version 7, daily, about 11 km grid, 1998-01-01 to 2026-09-28, with Kenya county boundaries (COD-AB).
  • County average: each grid cell weighted by the share of its area inside the county; areas of several counties weight each county equally. Single cell: the wettest grid cell with at least 50% of its area inside the county or area.
  • A threshold counts as reached on the first day the running total gets to it. Further days in the next 30 days count as the same occasion.
  • Floods: events in EM-DAT, the international disaster database, that started in October to December and whose location names the trigger's counties, 1998 to 2024. EM-DAT only includes events that meet its criteria (for example 100 or more people affected), often records one long event for a whole season, and can list many counties for one event. A flood counts as reached when the threshold was reached between 30 days before it began and its end.
  • How often: (number of seasons + 1) divided by the seasons reached, over the 28 seasons 1998-2025 (Weibull return period).
  • Checks: every count on this page, for both readings, was recalculated a second way from the saved rainfall grids and the raw EM-DAT file, and matched. Each flood matched to a county was checked against the EM-DAT location text.
  • The county rainfall matches the team's database copy of IMERG (correlation 0.9989).
