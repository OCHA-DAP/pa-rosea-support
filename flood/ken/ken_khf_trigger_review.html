Kenya flood triggers: how often the rainfall thresholds are reached

The Kenya Humanitarian Fund's RA2 allocation (September 2026) sets aside USD 4 million for anticipatory action ahead of El Niño floods. This page looks at how often the rainfall thresholds in its partner triggers have been reached since 1998, in the counties they were written for and in the other ASAL counties the allocation names.

Different data source: results may varyThe triggers are written on forecasts from KMSA (Kenya Meteorological Service Authority). This page uses NASA's IMERG satellite rainfall estimates instead. Totals from the two sources differ, so the dates and counts here may not match what KMSA data would give.

At a glance

  • Mandera, Wajir and Marsabit (150 mm): Reached in 5 of 28 years in at least one of the three counties. It was reached for 5 of 10 recorded floods in Wajir, 1 of 12 in Mandera and 1 of 11 in Marsabit.
  • Isiolo and Samburu (70 mm): Reached in 23 of 28 years, and for 10 of 12 recorded floods. At 100 mm: 18 of 28 years.
  • Garissa (40 mm rainfall part): Reached in 19 of 28 years, and for 12 of 24 recorded floods in Garissa, Tana River or Dadaab.
  • All triggers together: At least one was reached in 24 of 28 years.
  • Other counties: Applied to the county average in each of the eight ASAL counties named in the allocation, 150 mm in 7 days was reached in 0 to 7 of 28 years, and 70 mm in 7 days in 16 to 25 of 28 years.

What this page does not cover

  • Whether KMSA forecasts would have predicted these totals. This page uses observed rainfall.
  • The river-level triggers: Garissa Bridge above 5.1 m (Kenya Red Cross Society), Flood Alert levels at Garissa, Hola and Garsen (Dadaab partners), and the Danish Refugee Council trigger for Darika, which has no rainfall amount.
  • Where in a county the rainfall must fall. The triggers do not say, so this page uses the county average. Measured at the wettest spot in each county instead, 150 mm in 7 days is reached in 25 or more of 28 years.
Method and data
  • Rainfall: NASA IMERG Late Run version 7, daily, about 11 km grid, 1998-01-01 to 2026-09-28, averaged over Kenya county boundaries (COD-AB).
  • A threshold counts as reached on the first day the running total gets to it. Further days in the next 30 days count as the same occasion.
  • Floods: events in EM-DAT, the international disaster database, whose location names the trigger's counties, 1998 to 2024. EM-DAT only includes events that meet its criteria (for example 100 or more people affected), often records one long event for a whole season, and can list many counties for one event. A flood counts as reached when the threshold was reached between 30 days before it began and its end.
  • How often: (number of years + 1) divided by the years reached, over the 28 full years 1998-2025 (Weibull return period).
  • Checks: every count on this page was recalculated a second way, from the county rainfall series and the raw EM-DAT file, and matched. Each flood matched to a county was checked against the EM-DAT location text.
  • The county rainfall matches the team's database copy of IMERG (correlation 0.9989). The one difference that changes a count: Mandera in 2000 is 149.6 mm here and 150.7 mm in the database, so Mandera reaches 150 mm in 1 or 2 years depending on the copy used.
