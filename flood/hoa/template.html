Flood timing in October–December seasons with El Niño and a positive IOD

Start month of EM-DAT floods in seasons with both an El Niño and a positive Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) compared with other seasons, seasonal rainfall against recorded floods since 1981, temperature against rainfall, and the 2026 season to 5 November.

Key findings

    Seasons with El Niño and a positive IOD

    Between 1981 and 2024, six October–December (OND) seasons met both conditions, El Niño (Niño3.4 ≥ +0.5 °C) and a positive IOD (Dipole Mode Index, DMI, ≥ +0.4 °C): 1994, 1997, 2006, 2018, 2019 and 2023. No positive-IOD season occurred without El Niño. Provisional 2026 values exceed both thresholds (Figure 1).

    Data and methods

    • Season. October–December (OND): the Deyr in Somalia, the short rains in Kenya and the October–December rains of southern and southeastern Ethiopia.
    • Season classification. Niño3.4 anomaly from NOAA CPC (ERSST v5, 1991–2020 base) and DMI from NOAA PSL (HadISST 1.1), both averaged over OND. Thresholds: El Niño, Niño3.4 ≥ +0.5 °C; La Niña, Niño3.4 ≤ −0.5 °C; positive IOD, DMI ≥ +0.4 °C. Thresholds are applied to unrounded values.
    • 2026 indices. The HadISST DMI series ends in May 2026. ENSO state is taken from the NOAA CPC Oceanic Niño Index (August–October 2026). DMI is computed from NOAA OISST v2.1 daily anomalies (relative to 1971–2000) as the mean over 10°S–10°N, 50–70°E minus the mean over 10°S–0°, 90–110°E, to 7 November 2026.
    • Floods. EM-DAT (CRED, UCLouvain) flood events in Ethiopia, Kenya and Somalia with a start month from September to December, 1981–2024. Each country-season is counted once, at its earliest start month. September is included to capture floods that begin shortly before the OND rains. Two Ethiopian September events are located mainly outside the OND rainfall areas used here: 1999 (Benishangul-Gumuz) and 2005 (Tigray, Amhara and Oromia). EM-DAT lists no event in the three countries starting after May 2025, so the 2025 season is left out.
    • Rainfall. CHIRPS v3.0 (Funk et al. 2026) daily, averaged from 0.05° to 0.25° and area-weighted over cells where OND contributes at least 25% of annual rainfall (CHIRPS v3.0 monthly means, 1991–2020). CHIRPS v3.0 daily values are pentad totals divided among the days of the pentad: with ERA5 daily rainfall in the final data (the daily version that starts in 1981) and with IMERG in the preliminary data, so 2026 values from 1 October use a different daily split from earlier seasons, with the same pentad totals. CHC notes that onset dates can differ between the two splits. Onset is computed per cell by anomalous accumulation from 1 September (Liebmann et al. 2012; Dunning et al. 2016) and averaged over the same cells; cells without a detected onset are assigned the last day of the record. The 2026 comparison with earlier seasons uses rainfall from 1 September to 5 November in each season, as a percentage of the 1991–2020 mean for the same period.
    • Temperature. ERA5 2 m air temperature (Copernicus Climate Change Service): monthly means for 1981–2024, and for 2026 daily means to 31 October averaged by month. Values are interpolated to the centres of the CHIRPS cells and averaged over the same OND rainfall areas. Departures are from each month's linear trend over 1981–2024, which rises by °C per decade, so that warming over the period does not by itself make recent seasons look warm. The August–September value is the mean of the two monthly departures; a warm August–September is one above the trend. El Niño seasons are the 15 with OND Niño3.4 ≥ +0.5 °C, with or without a positive IOD. October rainfall is a percentage of the 1991–2020 October mean, and the wettest and driest Octobers are ranked by it (). Recent ERA5 months are preliminary (ERA5T) and are occasionally revised.
    • 2026 reports. Selected from the 53 ReliefWeb reports tagged flood with Ethiopia, Kenya or Somalia as primary country, 1 September to 9 November 2026; retained reports describe observed or expected flooding, river levels or an imminent-flood operation (FAO SWALIM river advisories, WHO, IRC, Radio Ergo, IFRC and FEWS NET). No report of OND 2026 flooding in Ethiopia was found. ReliefWeb lists no flood disaster event for the three countries this season.
    • Limitations. Six seasons met both conditions; counts describe these seasons and the other and are not probabilities. EM-DAT includes an event only if it meets at least one entry criterion (10 or more deaths, 100 or more people affected, a declared state of emergency or a call for international assistance), and its records may be less complete for earlier years.
